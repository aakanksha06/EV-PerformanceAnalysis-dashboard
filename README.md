🚗 Electric Vehicle (EV) Data Analysis & Market Trends Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing the growth, adoption trends, geographical distribution, and technological breakdown of Electric Vehicles (EVs).

🔗Live Interactive Dashboard: [View on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/aakanksha.sah1579/viz/EVDataAnalysis_17786665411720/Dashboard1)


📌 Project Overview

The global automotive market is rapidly shifting toward sustainable mobility. This project provides exploratory data analysis (EDA) and dynamic visualizations to understand the evolving landscape of electric vehicles.
The dashboard tracks key performance indicators (KPIs) and provides actionable insights into market penetration, manufacturer dominance, range capabilities, and regional adoption.

🎯 Key Objectives & Questions Answered

- **Adoption Growth:** How has EV adoption trended over model years?
- **Vehicle Type Distribution:** What is the market share ratio between **Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs)** and **Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs)**?
- **Leading Manufacturers:** Which automakers dominate the EV space by total vehicle volume and model diversity?
- **Range & Efficiency:** What is the average electric range across vehicle types and manufacturers?
- **Geographic Concentration:** Which states/counties lead in EV registrations?
- **Policy & Eligibility:** What proportion of vehicles qualify for Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) incentives?

📊 Dashboard Highlights & Metrics

- **Total Vehicles** - Total volume of registered electric vehicles in the dataset
- **Average Electric Range** - Average distance (in miles/km) vehicles can travel on pure electric charge
- **BEV vs. PHEV Share** - Percentage and count breakdown by powertrain technology
- **CAFV Eligibility** - Classification based on clean fuel incentives and tax rebate compliance


Visualizations Included:
1. **Time-Series Analysis:** EV population growth by Model Year.
2. **Top Makes & Models:** Bar chart/treemap of dominant manufacturers (Tesla, Chevrolet, Nissan, Ford, etc.).
3. **Geographic Distribution:** Heat map / symbol map showing adoption density across regions.
4. **Powertrain Breakdown:** Donut / Pie chart comparing BEV vs. PHEV market share.
5. **CAFV Status Breakdown:** Distribution of incentive-eligible vs. non-eligible vehicles.


🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Tableau Public / Tableau Desktop
- **Data Source:** Electric Vehicle Population Dataset
- **Techniques Applied:**
  - Data Cleaning & Profiling
  - Calculated Fields & Level of Detail (LOD) Expressions
  - Interactive Parameters & Filters (Year, Make, EV Type)
  - Custom KPI Scorecards & Dashboard UI Layout Design


🚀 How to Interact with the Dashboard

1. Visit the live link on [Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/aakanksha.sah1579/viz/EVDataAnalysis_17786665411720/Dashboard1).
2. Use the **top/side filter panels** to slice data by **Model Year**, **Vehicle Type (BEV/PHEV)**, or **Manufacturer**.
3. Hover over maps and charts for detailed tooltips and granular vehicle specifications.
